Section 1
North Star Dashboard
The full vision: the locked Cash Flow + Filtered View card pair is replaced by four filter-reactive KPI tiles — Income/Spending/Cash Flow carry a sparkline and a vs-prior-month delta (Cash Flow flips red when negative — sample data happens to be positive). Then Spending by Category Trend and Cash Flow Trend full-width, legends on the bottom, grouping buttons (Years / Quarters / Months / None — Years and Quarters only appear when the data spans more than one). None on the category chart renders a part-to-whole strip + ranked bars. Unstack flips the stacked columns into per-category lines (original colors; click a legend entry to focus one). Compare years (pivot with per-year axis sub-labels and cluster dividers) appears for Months/Quarters grouping when the span exceeds one year; Unstack and Compare are mutually exclusive. Hover a stacked column: the tooltip anchors to the column, lists every category, bolds the hovered one, and appends a total. The search box and filter chip above are decorative only. New charts follow: Fixed vs Variable (same grouping controls), Spending Seasonality, Expense Volatility, and the Subscription Audit.

Section 2
Density Guard — 24-Bucket Paging
Charts display at most 24 buckets per page. When the chosen grouping produces more (this demo's data spans 48 months with Months selected), pager links appear — no scrollbars. The default page is the most recent 24 and Prev/Next moves the window a full set at a time. The Seasonality heatmap pages by the same rule. The north star's 16-month span never triggers either pager.

Decision Log
Forks compared in round 1 labs, settled in review. The north star above embodies every pick.
  • Cross-year comparisonPeriod-of-year pivotInterleaved single axis judged too noisy; pivot gains a two-row axis (period + year) and cluster dividers. Years alternate full/faded color (newest full) — no descending ramp, no year caption; legend clicks toggle a category across all years.
  • None grouping (category)Strip + ranked barsReplaced the earlier doughnut pick after the gallery review — part-to-whole strip on top, ranked rows with name / $ / % inline; close shares are unreadable on a wheel.
  • KPI tilesSparklines + deltasIncome/Spending/Cash Flow get a monthly sparkline and a vs-prior-month delta (Spending's colors inverted: up = red). Recurring-share meter rejected — derivable from Spending's Fixed/month line.
  • SeasonalityRow-normalized heatmapSingle-hue red ramp, transparent → solid (green→red rejected: worst CVD pair, and a low month isn't "good"). 24-column pages like every other chart.
  • Per-category lines"Unstack" toggle on the trend chartNot a separate section — a checkbox beside Compare (mutually exclusive with it). Lines keep original category colors; legend click focuses one, rest go gray.
  • Fixed vs Variable controlsFull paritySame grouping pills + Compare years as the two main trends; None = two total columns.
  • Diverging cash flow (gallery)RejectedIncome-up/spending-down columns not adopted; the grouped-series form stays.
  • Subscription auditTable + inline barsCancel-this-specific-thing needs cadence + exact numbers; top 10 with an overflow row keeps the annual total honest.
  • Volatility & Audit layoutSplit rowStacks to full width under 1000px (mobile).
  • Too many columns24-bucket pagesPrev/Next pager links instead of scrollbars; newest page is the default.
  • Tooltips (stacked/grouped)Column-anchored, bold hovered rowPosition freezes per column; only the bolded row follows the mouse; total (or net) appended.
